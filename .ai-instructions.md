## Critical Architectural Constraints
- **Multiplatform Architecture**: Maximize logic in `sharedUI/src/commonMain`.
- **Layer Direction**: `presentation -> domain` and `data -> domain`.
- **ViewModel**: Use `androidx.lifecycle.ViewModel` in `commonMain`.
- **Navigation**: Use **Navigation 3** (Compose Multiplatform).
- **Mocking**: No reflection-based mocking (no MockK/Mockito). Use fakes or Ktor MockEngine.
- **Utility Files**: ❌ Forbidden naming (Utils, Helpers, etc.). Use domain-specific names.
